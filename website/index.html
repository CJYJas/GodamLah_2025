Public Kiosk Mode

Healthcare access without a smartphone

Unique Feature - Our Key Differentiator

IC + Voice = Instant Access

Our system can be deployed on public kiosks in hospitals, clinics, and government offices. Users simply insert their IC (MyKad) and use voice recognition to authenticate - no smartphone or password required. This is the simplest solution for digital inclusion.

Step 1: Insert MyKad

Kiosk reads IC chip and retrieves your profile

Step 2: Voice Authentication

Speak a simple phrase to verify your identity

Step 3: Access Healthcare

View records, book appointments, refill medications
