The Problem: 4 Pillars of Exclusion

Digital Complexity

Too many separate government apps; onboarding is confusing.

Sensory Exclusion

Weak support for visually/hearing-impaired; limited VoiceOver.

Geographic Divide

Low-bandwidth areas struggle; offline access is missing.

Data Fragmentation

ICs cannot sync health data nationwide across clinics/hospitals.

Solution: MyHealth Super-App

Unified App

Combine MySejahtera + MyUbat; single login, single dashboard.

Adaptive Modes

Elderly (VoiceOver, large text), Normal, Rural (offline + SMS).

Future IC Sync

Nationwide health record spine linked to IC for verified data.

Adaptive Modes

Elderly / Easy Mode

  • • Full VoiceOver & screen-reader support
  • • Large tap targets and simplified flows
  • • High-contrast, dyslexia-friendly palette

Normal Mode

  • • Fast navigation, unified records
  • • Appointment + refill flows in 2 taps
  • • Personalized reminders and alerts

Rural Mode

  • • Offline caching + low-bandwidth assets
  • • SMS fallback for critical reminders
  • • Optimized for intermittent connectivity

Tech Stack & Architecture

Backend & Data

  • • Python FastAPI for secure, high-perf APIs
